Privacy Policy

Last updated: [09/02/2026]

What this Site does not do

PowerPlay does not have user accounts, does not ask visitors for their name, email, phone number, or any other personal information, and does not sell or share any data about visitors, because it does not collect any in the first place.

What this Site does store

When you accept the age/terms notice on your first visit, your browser saves a small note locally (using a browser feature called "local storage") recording that you accepted, and when. This stays on your device only — it is never sent to us or to any server, and contains no personal information, just a yes/no flag and a timestamp. It's there so you're not shown the notice again every time you visit. You can clear it any time via your browser's site settings, which will show you the notice again on your next visit.

The staff login system

PowerPlay hosts use a separate, password-protected page to update picks and the leaderboard. This part of the system does process IP addresses — but only for security purposes, not to identify or track site visitors generally:

  • Failed login attempts are counted by IP address so that repeated incorrect attempts can be temporarily blocked, protecting the login from being guessed.
  • This information is kept only as long as needed for that security purpose (typically an hour or less) and is not linked to any visitor identity, sold, or used for any other purpose.
  • This only applies to the staff login page — it has nothing to do with, and does not track, general visitors browsing the public site.

Your privacy rights

Because this Site collects so little (see above), there's very little data to access, correct, or delete for any individual visitor. If you'd still like to make a request — for example under the CCPA, GDPR, or a similar law that applies to you — contact us at the email below and we'll respond. If you're a PowerPlay host with a staff login, you can request correction or deletion of security logs tied to your access the same way.

Data retention

The local-storage acceptance flag stays on your device until you clear it or accept a newer version of the Terms. Staff login security data (failed-attempt counts, temporary IP blocks) is automatically deleted on a short timer, typically within an hour, as described above.

International visitors

This Site is hosted and operated with U.S.-based services (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare), which may process requests through servers located in different countries as part of how those services normally operate. If you're visiting from outside the United States, your information (to the limited extent described above) may be processed in the U.S. or elsewhere.

Third-party services this Site uses

This Site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads content from a couple of outside services in order to work:

  • YouTube — short videos are embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which limits tracking compared to standard YouTube embeds. YouTube (Google) may still process technical information (like your IP address) when a video is played, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — this Site loads its typeface from Google's font service, which involves your browser making a request to Google's servers.
  • GitHub Pages — as with any website host, GitHub may log basic technical request information (like IP address) as part of running the server. See GitHub's privacy statement.

This Site does not currently use any analytics or advertising tracking (e.g. Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel). [If that changes in the future, this section needs to be updated to name the specific tool added and what it tracks.]

Children's privacy

This Site is intended for adults of legal gambling age only (see the Terms of Use & Disclaimer) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what's collected or how it's used, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also re-prompt the age/terms notice, which links here.
